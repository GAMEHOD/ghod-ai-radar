GHOD AI Radar

Instant alerts when an app or AI gains control of your phone

  • Alerts you as soon as an app gains Accessibility, Device admin, or Notification access, or when ADB or developer options are turned on
  • Free scam app scan: compares your apps with a list of reported scam apps and flags apps named like banks or government agencies
  • Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads.
